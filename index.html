About

I'm a data science master's student at NYU, working on the science of deep learning. My current focus: deep networks can memorize entirely random labels — and that memorization is not a dead end. It transfers, it accelerates later learning, and it has structure worth mapping.

Before New York I studied data science in Zhoushan, an island city in China — B.S. at Zhejiang Ocean University, 2025.

Research
Working paper · Targeting a NeurIPS workshop · 2026

Scaling Laws and Transfer Effects in Random Label Memorization

Yimiao Yu & Florentin Guth — NYU Center for Data Science

Pretraining on one random-label assignment accelerates memorizing a different one. The effect survives random-pixel inputs, requires a consistent input–output mapping, and is carried mainly by convolutional weights.
