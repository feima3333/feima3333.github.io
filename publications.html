Localizing Transfer Between Memorization Tasks NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Neural Network Artifacts as a New Data Modality

Yimiao Yu & Florentin Guth

Memorizing one set of random labels speeds up memorizing another: after a short warm-up, each epoch of pre-training saves about one epoch of fine-tuning. Early in pre-training, an epoch on pure noise can even save more than one downstream epoch. We decompose the speedup into a magnitude-driven part in the last layer, explained by weight-norm growth, and a structure-driven part in the convolutional layers, partly carried by their covariance.
